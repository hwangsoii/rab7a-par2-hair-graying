# Historical reconstruction archive, read-only evidence

These files are byte-identical copies of the 1 October 2026 reconstruction
plans, scripts and selected aggregate records. `SOURCE_SHA256.json` records
their original hashes. They preserve changes over time; statements such as
"HOLD" or "no P values" refer to their dated checkpoint, not current release
status. The root REPRODUCIBILITY describes the current analysis and later
nominal-P addition. Initial-plan outcome script 04 was not executed; amended
script 08 produced the recorded descriptive results once.

This archive is not the standalone Figure S2B entry point. Use
`supplementary_code/external_cohort/01_make_Figure_S2B.R` with released source
tables to reproduce the revised figure/statistics.

The historical preprocessing scripts expect the original relative workspace
layout (including `OneDrive_2025-09-11/Data/cell discovery/...`), downloaded
Zenodo matrices, source manifests and large intermediate objects. Original
barcodes, selected-cell membership files, full objects and full matrices are
deliberately omitted here. Thus historical freeze gates cannot be executed
from this minimal archive alone. Do not remove or fabricate those gates.
A fresh complete count-to-figure run needs a separately documented setup,
the public source inputs and review of the resulting population. This release
does not claim such a clean-environment rerun was performed.

The archived plans disclose the old exploratory analysis; it is not treated
as a valid independent replication. Public donor labels in these records
come from the published third-party study, not our study participants.
